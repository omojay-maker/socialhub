Terms of Service — 1TechLink Social Hub

Last updated: October 2026 · Operated by 1TechLink (demo deployment)

1. What this app does

1TechLink Social Hub lets you connect your Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok and X accounts, compose posts once, and publish or schedule them to those platforms. It also shows notifications and analytics from the accounts you connect.

2. Your accounts and content

  • You must own (or be authorized to manage) every social account you connect.
  • You are responsible for the content you publish through the app and for complying with each platform's terms.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and are used only to act on your behalf as you direct (publish, schedule, read notifications/analytics).
  • Disconnecting an account in the app revokes its use here; you may also revoke access from within each platform.

3. Acceptable use

Do not use the app to spam, harass, mislead, or violate any law or platform policy. We may suspend access for misuse.

4. Availability

This deployment is a demonstration environment provided as-is, without warranties. Features that depend on third-party platform APIs may change or stop working when those platforms change their APIs.
