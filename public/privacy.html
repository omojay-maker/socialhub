Privacy Policy — 1TechLink Social Hub

Last updated: October 2026 · Operated by 1TechLink (demo deployment)

1. Data we handle

  • Account data you provide: name, email and login credentials for this app.
  • Connected social accounts: platform account identifiers, profile names, follower counts and OAuth access/refresh tokens (stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM).
  • Content you create: post text, hashtags, scheduling choices and uploaded media.
  • Operational data: activity logs (who did what, when) and rate-limit counters.

2. How we use it

Only to operate the service you asked for: publishing/scheduling posts, syncing notifications and analytics, and keeping the audit trail. We do not sell personal data, run ads against it, or share it except with the platforms you explicitly connect (acting strictly on your behalf).

3. Storage and security

  • Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text); OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • Traffic is served over HTTPS with Secure, HttpOnly session cookies.
  • Demo deployments may reset stored data on redeploy.

4. Your control

Disconnecting a social account stops all use of its token here. Contact your workspace administrator to export or delete your data.

5. Contact

Privacy questions: contact your 1TechLink workspace administrator.
